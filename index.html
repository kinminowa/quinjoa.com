content

Helped Build a Seamless Content Workflow

I joined this founder as his first hire, before any content system existed. Together we built a content pipeline from scratch, since content was already his primary driver of leads and needed a real process behind it.

I write the content briefs. When the founder has his own idea, we collaborate on it to make sure it still hits the ICP. I manage the Notion workflow end to end, brief the editor on what needs to happen, and keep every asset organized in Frame.io.

That structure is what keeps the pipeline moving on schedule. Content ships on the date it's supposed to, because speed against competitors is one of the advantages we're protecting.

Once an editor finishes a piece, it comes to me first for review, then to the client for final sign-off. If anything doesn't land with him personally, it goes back to the editor before it ships.

Impact

  • Maintained a consistent publishing workflow with no missed ship dates
  • Supported inbound lead generation through content
  • Contributed to closing clients since joining
  • Improved collaboration between founder and editor
  • Supported outbound lead generation
